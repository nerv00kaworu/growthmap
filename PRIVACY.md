## Local data

GrowthMap is local-first. Projects, nodes, relations, content blocks, branches, implementation evidence, provider profiles, trial state, License state, managed backups, and operational logs are stored on the user’s device. Provider secrets are stored through the configured local secret mechanism and are not returned by the normal API.

## Data sent externally

GrowthMap sends content externally only when the user deliberately invokes an optional AI/provider feature configured by that user. The destination, processing, retention, and charges are controlled by the selected third-party provider and its terms. GrowthMap v1 has no product analytics or advertising tracker by default.

Manual purchases are processed through PayPal or Base. PayPal processes information under its own notice. Base transactions are public blockchain records. To verify payment and issue/recover a License, the Creator may retain contact email, License display name, payment rail, transaction ID or transaction hash, amount/currency, payment evidence, sale ordinal, License/revocation records, recovery identifier, operator audit events, and support communications.

## Purpose and retention

Data is used to operate the local product; verify entitlement; prevent duplicate or fraudulent issuance; provide recovery, refunds, revocation, security, and support; and comply with legal/accounting obligations. Payment/License/audit records may be retained while the License remains valid and afterward where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, disputes, security, tax, or legal duties. Public blockchain records cannot be deleted by GrowthMap.

## Sharing

The Creator does not sell personal data. Data may be disclosed to service providers the user chooses, payment providers, infrastructure providers used for support or releases, or authorities when legally required. No private signing key, provider secret, or desktop session token is intentionally included in distributed packages.

## Security and rights

GrowthMap uses signed Licenses, local protected storage, strict desktop sessions, and fail-closed checks, but no system is perfectly secure. Users should keep backups and protect their devices. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of retained operator records by emailing the contact above. Some records may be retained where legally required or necessary to prevent duplicate License issuance or fraud.

## Children and changes

GrowthMap is not directed to children who cannot legally consent in their jurisdiction. Material notice changes will be included with a later release. Mandatory local privacy rights prevail over conflicting terms.
